Dota Translator

Privacy

Dota Translator uses email-code accounts and hosted translation. Here is what leaves your PC and what we keep.

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Who is responsible

Dota Translator is operated by Kristjan Ranna in Estonia. Contact support@dotatranslator.live for support or a request about your data.

Your account

We store your email, account creation time, trial deadline, subscription expiry, payment references and a hash identifying the PC bound to your account. The machine identifier itself is not sent. Login codes and session tokens are stored as hashes on the server. Resend sends the login email.

Your chat

Only lines needing translation and the language requested are sent through our Netcup server to Google's paid Gemini API. We do not save chat text in server logs or the account database. A temporary in-memory cache remembers repeated translations. Text you choose to send with Ctrl+Enter is also sent for translation. Google's Gemini API terms apply to its processing.

Usage counts

While Dota is in front, the signed-in app sends a heartbeat about once a minute. The private stats page counts distinct accounts seen in the last three minutes, daily and monthly activity, translated lines and API usage. These are app activity observations, not proof that a match was played. Your network address is used for rate limits and abuse prevention.

Payments

Translator access costs EUR 7 for three months after the three-day trial. Card checkout is handled by Swapped; direct crypto payments use USDC on Polygon. Payment providers process their checkout details under their own policies. We retain order and confirmed transaction references to grant access and prevent the same payment being used twice. Skin Changer and Translator purchases grant access to their respective products separately.

What stays on your PC

  • Your settings and encrypted sign-in session, protected with Windows encryption when available.
  • said.json, the translated replies you sent, so they can be reused or corrected locally.
  • Small screen captures used to identify heroes or the chat channel are processed locally and discarded.

How the app works

Chat comes from Valve's Game State Integration feed. The app does not read Dota's memory or inject code. It presses keys only when you ask to send a translated reply. Updates come from GitHub; missing hero portraits can come from Valve's image server. API credentials remain on our server.

This website

The website uses Google Analytics for page visits and button clicks. GitHub Pages and Cloudflare serve the site and process connection information. These website analytics are separate from the app's account activity counts.

Retention and your requests

Account and payment records are kept to provide access, reconcile payments and handle support. Hashed device trial history prevents repeated free trials. Login codes expire after 15 minutes; sessions expire after 90 days without use and can be ended by signing out. Contact support to request access, correction or deletion of your account data, or ask about records retained for payments and trial abuse prevention.

We do not sell your personal data. The owner-only stats page is password protected.
